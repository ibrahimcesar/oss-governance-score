# Exploratory report — OSS Governance Score (post-review)

## 1. Verdict in three sentences

Nothing in the 24 ideas is groundbreaking in the sense of a new phenomenon or method: every load-bearing component has a published antecedent (Saisana 2005, El Emam 2001, Rigby 2016, Elazhary 2019, Chakraborti 2024, Scorecard's own Gerrit/Phabricator parser), and at n = 100 (Scorecard n = 53, toy n = 5) the study cannot overturn any of them. What is realistically achievable before the defence (probability ≈ 0.9) is a set of instrument-level results that harden the thesis against the one question the 20 critiques left open — "what does your score add beyond size, and what can it not see?" — using data already on disk. The only path to a genuinely new contribution is post-defence and prospective: a pre-registered second wave in July 2027 testing Asparouhova's typology as trajectories and the score's predictive validity on an unconditioned outcome (P ≈ 0.35 of a clean positive), plus a "scope conditions and gameability of platform-visible governance indices" methods paper that would also indict Scorecard (P ≈ 0.5 of a strong short paper, ≈ 0.2 that the framing is adopted).

## 3. Tier B — the post-defence paper

## 5. Refuted / deprioritised

- **C2 fsQCA/NCA:** with 7 binaries, 84% of truth-table rows are remainders and only 4 rows pass consistency ≥ 0.80 ∧ freq ≥ 2; "sufficiency" equals the stratum base rate (mean Y1 ≈ 0.81 in fed+club); Y1 is ρ = 0.93 with the score and 0.83 with the classifier variable.
- **C5 percolation/gluing defect:** cache has no file lists and federation histories are truncated (linux 600/75,372), so the prototype's 3 federations are the ceiling; DOA needs full history; the archetype "fingerprint" is the stratification definition; Rigby 2016 / Cosentino 2015 / Jabrayilzade 2022 did it at larger n.
- **C6 as a theory test:** the seven "predictions" already carry p-values from the same data (not pre-registration); P1/P3/P4 are mechanically implied by the classification inputs; only 11% of toy response edges fall inside the 12-month window, so the maint/peer split is mis-labelled former maintainers.
- **C7 regime typology and Tier 2:** F contains three D1 items and U contains a D3 metric, so score-by-regime ordering (34.8/62.1/67.6/78.7) is arithmetic; `author_association` measures org-membership visibility (openai/codex → "ceremonial" with 0.00 insider share); Elazhary 2019 and Chakraborti 2024 already published the phenomenon at n = 53 and 208.
- **C8 Layers B–C:** community axis = contributor axis (92/100 agree with the archetype split); GMM at k = 4 with full covariance ≈ 100 parameters on n = 100; CoC/CONTRIBUTING bodies are not cached; P3/P5/P7 near-constant.
- **C10 Tier 1 and backdated Tier 2:** part–whole with D3 (same 50-PR list), collider on `author_association`, informative censoring; backdating estimates P(T0 | T1) on a T1-stratified sample, so the transition matrix is not identifiable. Replaced by B1.
- **C1 implication mining, C3 Kan framing, C4 as a contribution:** textbook machinery (Ganter & Wille; Manski bounds; El Emam protocol) — keep the computations (A1, A3, A4), drop the novelty claims.
- **Staggered-adoption DiD:** endogenous adoption, small nudged subsample, 12 days at p = 0.45; only after B1 shows precedence.

## 6. Category theory — what is load-bearing

Ranking by what the formulation buys that a plain statistic does not:

1. **FCA Galois connection (C1).** Buys: the exact sense in which the score is compensatory — a monotone map from the concept lattice to [0, 100] that is a linear extension of an order which is not a chain — and the information-loss count (A4). Textbook (Ganter & Wille 1999; Duquenne 1995); one sentence in 4.2.1, cited, not claimed.
2. **Transition quiver (C10/B1).** Buys: the only empirical test of CotC's six declared arrows — which occur at 12 months, and whether D-profiles are monotone along them (the "Main Theorem" H_VSM∘F = H_OSS reduces to this and is already contradicted by CotC's own 0.769 > 0.474 along Fed→Stadium). Needs wave 2.
3. **Product order / monotone collapse (C8 plane, C3 dominance).** Buys: incomparable pairs made visible (fzf vs linux). The 4% comparability figure on 25 coordinates is a dimensionality artefact — report only on the 5 sub-scores (38.7%) or against a column-permutation null.
4. **Kan extensions Lan/Ran for the missing rule (C3).** Exact, but Lan = impute 0 and Ran = impute 1 are Manski bounds; the real finding is that the omit-and-renormalise rule is weighted-mean imputation, non-universal. Footnote, cite Shiebler 2022.
5. **Sheaf/cosheaf typing of metrics (C5).** Buys a compositionality lemma for catalogue design (counts and sets compose across windows/modules; ratios, entropies and medians do not) and the honest verdict for the CotC theory file: the sheaf of commit records is flabby (Čech H¹ = 0), derived metrics are not presheaves, so the "gluing defect" is the whole empirical residue of Prop 4.3.1 — and it is window sensitivity under another name.

Decorative, do not put in the thesis: Gödel lattice for fsQCA, the Openers ← Threads → Responders span, the locus fibration, apposition of K_form/K_use, "Governance ⊣ Autonomy", terminal Stadium (not terminal — uniqueness fails with the Fed⇄Stadium cycle), all Čech Hⁿ of a Set-valued presheaf. The separate deep-dive gives the formal sketch; for the defence, items 1 and 3 earn at most one paragraph each, and everything else belongs in the categories-of-the-commons repo as a corrections note.
